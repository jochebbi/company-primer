# NextEra Energy | Company Primer

**Company Research & Financial Overview**

## Overview

This project presents a company primer on NextEra Energy (NYSE: NEE), a major US energy company with operations spanning regulated electricity utilities and energy infrastructure.

The objective is to provide a concise overview of the company's business model, financial characteristics, market positioning and key investment considerations.

## Research Areas

- **Company Profile:** Business model, principal operations and revenue drivers.
- **Industry Overview:** US electricity markets, energy infrastructure and renewable energy developments.
- **Financial Overview:** Key financial metrics and historical performance.
- **Investment Considerations:** Growth opportunities, industry trends and relevant business risks.
